Nerdbase privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, streak, mistakes, saved problems, settings and name are stored on your device. Nerdbase has no account database.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in, Apple gives Nerdbase a stable identifier so scan limits can be counted per person. Nerdbase does not receive your password.

Scanning a problem

When you scan or type a problem for online solving, the photo or text is sent to Nerdbase's server and on to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the solution. The photo is not stored by Nerdbase. The server keeps only a daily count of scans per user. Derivatives typed in the app are solved on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. Nerdbase does not see your payment details.

Camera and notifications

The camera is used only to scan a problem. Notifications are reminders you choose; you can turn them off in Settings.

Deleting your data

In Profile, Delete all data removes everything on the device and asks the server to delete your usage records.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/nilslilja/nerdbase-site.
